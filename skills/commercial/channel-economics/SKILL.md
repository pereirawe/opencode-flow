# Channel Economics Skill

Design partner economics that drive desired behaviors.

## Margin & Incentive Design

### Partner Margin by Tier
| Tier | Standard Margin | Performance Rebate | Effective Margin |
|------|----------------|-------------------|------------------|
| Silver | 20% | 0% | 20% |
| Gold | 25% | 5% | 30% |
| Platinum | 25% | 10% | 35% |

### Incentive Types
- **Deal registration**: 5-10% extra for sourced deals
- **Performance rebate**: Quarterly or annual based on volume attainment
- **MDF (Market Development Funds)**: Co-op marketing dollars
- **SPIFs**: Short-term incentives for specific products/behaviors
- **Renewal commission**: Incentivize renewals and expansions

## Partner Profitability Analysis

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Partner CAC | Partner program cost / partner-sourced customers | <50% of direct CAC |
| Partner ROI | (Partner revenue - program cost) / program cost | >3x |
| Revenue per partner | Total partner revenue / active partners | Increasing YoY |
| Margin erosion | Discounted deals / total partner deals | <20% |

## Channel ROI Calculator
- Program costs: headcount, MDF, incentives, tools, events
- Partner revenue: sourced, influenced, attributed
- Net channel contribution: revenue - program costs
- Channel efficiency ratio: partner revenue / program cost
