# Proposals Skill

Create compelling proposals that clearly articulate value.

## Proposal Structure

1. **Executive Summary** — their problem, your understanding, your solution, ROI, investment
2. **Situation Analysis** — demonstrate deep understanding of their context
3. **Solution Overview** — approach, methodology, deliverables, timeline
4. **Detailed Scope** — Phases, milestones, what's in/out of scope
5. **Commercial Terms**

   | Item | Description | Investment |
   |------|-------------|------------|
   | Setup & implementation | One-time | $X,XXX |
   | Annual subscription | Year 1-3 | $X,XXX/yr |
   | Additional services | Optional add-ons | $X,XXX |
   | **Total investment** | | **$X,XXX** |

6. **ROI Section** — current state cost vs future state value, payback period
7. **Terms** — payment schedule, SLA, termination, renewal
8. **Next Steps** — acceptance process, onboarding timeline, key contacts

## Commercial Negotiation

### Before Negotiation
- Know your walk-away point (MAPA — Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement)
- Know their alternatives (what else can they do?)
- Know your value drivers (what's most valuable to them?)
- Set anchoring range (target, stretch, walk-away)

### During Negotiation
- **Concession strategy**: Trade, don't give — every concession gets something back
- **Silence**: After presenting price or terms, speak next only when they respond
- **Bundling**: Package concessions that cost you little but matter to them
- **Framing**: Present price in context of value delivered, not as cost

### After Negotiation
- Confirm all terms in writing immediately
- Document concessions and rationale for deal desk/approval
- Transition to onboarding with clear expectations set
