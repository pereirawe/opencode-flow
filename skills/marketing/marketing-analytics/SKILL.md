# Marketing Analytics Skill

Measure what matters and diagnose performance issues.

## KPI Framework

### Top-of-Funnel (Awareness)
- Impressions, reach, CPM
- Traffic by channel: organic, paid, social, referral, direct, email
- New vs returning visitors
- Content engagement: time on page, scroll depth, shares

### Middle-of-Funnel (Consideration)
- Lead generation: form fills, demo requests, content downloads
- Lead quality: MQL → SQL conversion rate
- Email metrics: open rate, CTR, list growth rate
- Engagement score: pages/session, frequency, recency

### Bottom-of-Funnel (Conversion)
- Conversion rate by channel and page
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Revenue attribution: first-touch, last-touch, multi-touch
- AOV, LTV, ROI by channel

### Retention
- Churn rate (monthly/quarterly/annual)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer Health Score

## Diagnostics Flow

### Traffic Drop
1. Check all channels — is it isolated or across the board?
2. Technical: site speed, server errors, robots.txt changes
3. SEO: ranking changes, algorithm updates, competitor moves
4. Paid: budget caps, creative fatigue, audience saturation
5. Seasonal: compare YoY, not MoM

### Conversion Drop
1. Funnel breakdown: which step lost traffic?
2. Page-level: recent changes to page, offer, or CTA
3. Segment analysis: which segment dropped most?
4. Technical: form errors, payment failures, redirect issues
5. External: competitor offers, pricing changes, market shifts

## Reporting Structure
- **Daily**: Channel performance, anomalies, budget pacing
- **Weekly**: Campaign comparison, trend analysis, quick wins
- **Monthly**: Full funnel report, RoAS, CAC/LTV, strategic insights
- **Quarterly**: Board-ready summary, competitive benchmarking, forecast
